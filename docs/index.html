The AI coach

A coach in your ear, not a spreadsheet

Every telemetry tool shows you charts. RideGuide is built around the thing the charts are for — getting faster — and puts a full crew on your radio while you drive:

  • Live coaching — braking too early, missed apexes, overheating fronts: spotted as it happens, with a fix, not a lecture. It knows when to shut up, too.
  • A spotter for close racing and a race engineer for flags, fuel, tires, weather and damage.
  • Post-lap corner analysis that ranks where your time is actually going.
  • Session debriefs, track guides and practice drills — grounded in your actual laps, never generic advice.
  • Pick your personality — supportive mentor, blunt race engineer, rally co-driver — with full text-to-speech, team-radio effects, and push-to-talk so you can talk back.

The cockpit

Everything is recorded, everything is live

Sessions land in a local database automatically — compare today against last month, per car, per track. Live gauges with 19 skin styles, charts, a track map that remembers every road you've travelled, opponent timing, and a g-g diagram, in fully customizable layouts.

An optional LAN server turns a phone or tablet into a DDU, timing screen or pit wall, and feeds OBS overlays for streamers — off by default, token-protected when on.

Pro-grade analysis

Find the tenths, corner by corner

Distance-aligned lap comparison, virtual sectors, theoretical best, and live delta to your reference laps. Corner detection ranks every corner by time lost — with brake, minimum-speed and exit-speed deltas — so you know exactly what to fix first.

Math channels with a safe expression engine, per-lap statistical reports, and export to CSV, Parquet, MoTeC .ld and shareable lap files when you want to go deeper.

Open worlds count too

A map that draws itself

Every road you travel is remembered and drawn, colored by surface — this is one player's actual Forza Horizon world, built up drive by drive. RideGuide detects when a free-roam loop becomes a circuit and times laps geometrically, so Horizon and BeamNG get lap timing even when the game reports none.

Tuning & setup

A garage that reads the game

RideGuide captures your tuning sheets and upgrade catalogs straight off the screen — per car, per game, no manual data entry. Native setup files for the sims that have them, and two-way digital tuning in BeamNG.

Describe the symptom — “understeer on corner exit” — and the setup engineer proposes ranked changes grounded in your car's real slider ranges, then lets lap-attributed A/B validation prove whether the change was actually faster.

Your AI, your rules

Bring your own AI — or none at all

RideGuide never resells AI access. There is no subscription because there is nothing to subscribe to.

Fully offline

A bundled local model runs coaching, debriefs and chat with zero internet and zero accounts. Runs on CPU by default — your game keeps the GPU.

Connect what you already have

Claude, Gemini, OpenAI-compatible endpoints, or a local Ollama server. RideGuide routes each job to the model best suited for it.

See every penny

An in-app cost panel meters anything you choose to spend, per feature. Running costs floor at $0 — cloud on accounts you already own, or fully local.

Verify your download — file checksum (SHA-256)

A checksum is a public fingerprint of the installer file — it is not a licence key or a secret. After downloading, run certutil -hashfile RideGuide-Setup-0.2.0.exe SHA256 in a terminal and compare: a match proves your download arrived intact and untampered.
